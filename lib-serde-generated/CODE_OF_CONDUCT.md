# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces of lib-serde-generated.

That includes the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other
channel used to develop the project. It also applies when representing the
project in public.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Keep discussions on topic and technical.
- Accept review feedback and give it constructively.
- Respect maintainers' decisions on scope and direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or bad-faith issues and pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject any contribution, comment, or issue that
violates this document, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for
repeated or severe violations.

## Reporting

Report violations by email to conduct@example.org.

Reports are handled confidentially. You will receive an acknowledgment within 72
hours.

Do not use this address for security issues; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
